Bio

I am a composer at the beginning of my journey, learning how to express myself through the piano. Rather than following a set path, I am letting each new piece act as an experiment — a search for a style that feels right. My music will be a documentation of that discovery.

I live near Stonehenge in the UK, and am preparing for a series of releases throughout 2026. This space serves as the primary hub for my latest projects and performance updates.

Music

Listen to my latest tracks and compositions below. My work is available on all major streaming platforms.

I aim to share new pieces roughly once a month, though I prefer to let the music dictate the timing. To join me as these pieces unfold, follow on Spotify for the latest releases.

My first piece, "Requiem", considers the loss of a lover through quiet reflection, and will be released 22 May.
