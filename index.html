Bio

Welcome. I am Nathaniel Hook, a pianist and composer dedicated to creating evocative, minimalist soundscapes. My music blends classical discipline with modern atmospheric textures, designed for deep listening and cinematic reflection.

Currently based in the UK, I am preparing for a series of releases throughout 2026. This space serves as the primary hub for my latest projects and performance updates.
